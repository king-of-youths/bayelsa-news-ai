# Bayelsa News AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Bayelsa News AI provides daily Bayelsa State news updates, summaries, and links to news sources through WhatsApp.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Bayelsa News AI, we may collect:

* Your WhatsApp phone number.
* Messages you send to the service.
* Your preferences relating to news updates.
* Information needed to deliver requested news updates.
* Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

* Deliver Bayelsa State news updates.
* Respond to questions and requests.
* Manage subscriptions.
* Improve the reliability and quality of the service.
* Prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access.
* Maintain and troubleshoot the service.

## 3. News Sources

Bayelsa News AI may provide links to articles published by third-party news organizations. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

## 4. WhatsApp

The service operates through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Your interaction with the service is subject to applicable WhatsApp and Meta policies.

## 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the service, such as hosting, database, automation, messaging, and artificial intelligence service providers. These providers receive only information necessary to provide their services and are expected to protect it appropriately.

We may also disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users, or others.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.
